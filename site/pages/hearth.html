How to give

Spark, Forge, Pass it on

Every gift goes straight from one wallet to another. There is no treasury, no middleman and no central wallet.

Spark

Gift an ember-maker

Someone posted a real #PrometheusEmber that moved you. If they added a Solana address to their post, send them a small gift. Reply to say thanks so it's visible.

Forge

Fuel a bigger project

Someone proposes something bigger in public: a community garden, a free tool, a workshop. Plan, budget, wallet, milestones. Gift a part when each milestone is shown.

Pass

Buy to give, not to hold

Get a little $PROMETHEUS with the intention of giving it away. The token becomes a way to carry the flame to someone who is using it well.

Forge proposals

How a Forge works

For projects too big for one ember. Anyone can propose one. Tenders check it before it is shared.

  1. ProposePost the plan publicly: what, why, budget, a wallet, and 2 to 4 milestones.
  2. CheckTenders confirm the proposer has given embers before and the plan is real.
  3. FuelHolders gift toward the first milestone only.
  4. ShowThe proposer posts proof of each milestone before the next is fuelled.

The Hearth rules

A gift is never a deal

  • Embers are never owed anything.An ember counts the same with or without a gift. Nobody does a deed "for" a gift.
  • Gifts come after the deed.Never in advance, never as a promise, never as a trade.
  • No begging, no rankings.Don't ask for gifts. No leaderboard of who received the most.
  • Public addresses only.Send only to an address posted in the public ember or Forge post. Never to an address from a DM.
  • No treasury.Nobody collects funds for the Loom. Anyone who asks you to send to "the community wallet" is a scammer.
  • Think about privacy.A public wallet can be traced. Use a separate wallet for gifts if you want to keep your main one private.
