Doing good well

Give like you mean it

  1. Ask what's needed. Help that someone asked for beats help that looks good in a photo.
  2. Do no harm. Plant only species native to your area, stay safe, and leave places better than you found them.
  3. Protect people's privacy. Don't post faces, names, addresses or screenshots of the people you helped without asking them first.
  4. Small and finished beats big and abandoned. One translated paragraph that's done is an ember. A promised book is not.
  5. Show, don't claim. A link, an action shot or a before and after. No exaggeration: the Loom only holds honest embers.
  6. Be open about AI. Say what the AI did and what you did. Check its facts, and never give it other people's personal details.
  7. Credit others. If someone helped or inspired you, name them.
  8. Expect nothing back. No rewards, no tokens for embers. The flame is the point.
Helping others do well

Tend each other's embers

  1. Reply to every ember within a day. Being seen is why people come back.
  2. Thank them specifically. "Your translation made Chapter 0 readable for my mum" beats "nice 🔥".
  3. Never shame a small ember. Five minutes counts. Everyone starts somewhere.
  4. Offer to join. "Want help with the next part?" turns one ember into two.
  5. Pair up newcomers. Match someone new with someone who has posted before, for their first ember.
  6. Suggest a next step, gently. Point to an idea from this page that builds on what they did.
  7. Check honestly, kindly. If an ember can't be verified, ask for proof in private before saying anything in public.
